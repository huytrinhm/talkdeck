Arrange four to six peer items in a regular grid. Repeat the same internal hierarchy in each item: label first, short explanation second, optional meaningful visual. Align comparable parts and keep gaps consistent. Use light grouping, not excessive borders or nested boxes. This works for features, examples, categories or people. The item count comes from the content; do not invent an extra item to complete a row. For unequal or sequential material, choose a different structure.
