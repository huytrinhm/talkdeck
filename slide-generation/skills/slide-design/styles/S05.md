Soft ivory background, dark brown-charcoal text, muted terracotta and sage accents. Pair a friendly serif heading with a readable humanist sans-serif body. Use gentle curves or lightly tinted groups when useful, with generous spacing and an approachable rhythm. Small organic shapes or simple line illustrations may add warmth, but avoid stickers, childish decoration and a card around every item. Keep charts, tables and code just as precise as in a formal style. Aim for welcoming, composed and tactile rather than ornamental.

Typography is an intent, not a font reference. Use only the currently resolved font package; do not invent family tokens or assume a serif/body pairing is available. Preserve existing typography when the requested pairing cannot be resolved.
