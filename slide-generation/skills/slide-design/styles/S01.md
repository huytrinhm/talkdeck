Warm off-white, near-black ink and a restrained forest-green accent. Use an elegant serif heading with a clean sans-serif body. Aim for a thoughtful journal or well-edited book: generous open space, strong left edges, delicate rules when they organize information, and very few enclosing boxes. Emphasis should come mainly from typography and spacing. Photographs, charts and diagrams remain clear and unembellished. Do not make dense content look like a decorative cover.

Typography is an intent, not a font reference. Use only the currently resolved font package; do not invent family tokens or assume a serif/body pairing is available. Preserve existing typography when the requested pairing cannot be resolved.
