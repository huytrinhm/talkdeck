Use warm white, near-black and a vivid vermilion accent. Choose heavy sans-serif display headings and straightforward sans-serif body text. Build energy through confident scale contrast, a small number of strong flat shapes and intentional asymmetry; keep reading order obvious. A solid accent field can support a short statement, but dense content needs a quiet reading surface. Avoid gradients, ornamental icons and many competing colors. The effect is a contemporary poster translated into a usable presentation, not a busy collage.
